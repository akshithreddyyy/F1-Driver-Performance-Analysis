# 🏎️ F1 2023 Season — Driver Performance Analysis

A personal project I built to get hands on with Python and data analysis. 
Being an F1 fan made this a lot more fun than analysing random datasets.

---

## 🎯 What I was trying to figure out

- Who actually dominated the 2023 season (spoiler: not close)
- Which drivers were consistent podium finishers vs. one hit wonders
- How the championship gap grew round by round throughout the season

---

## 📊 What's in the notebook

- **Points leaderboard** — total points per driver across the full season
- **Podium count** — who stood on the podium most frequently
- **Win distribution** — how race wins were spread across the grid
- **Championship progression** — cumulative points race by race for the top 5 drivers

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries

- Python
- Pandas — data cleaning and manipulation
- Matplotlib & Seaborn — visualisations
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 💡 A few things I found interesting

- Verstappen's points lead was basically unassailable by the halfway point of the season
- The gap between P1 and P2 in cumulative points was larger than the gap between P2 and P8
- A handful of drivers accounted for almost all the wins — the midfield rarely broke through
